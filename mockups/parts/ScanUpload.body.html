How pages are grouped
A routing code starts a new packet. Every page after it, up to the next code, belongs to that student. The code is on the front of the rubric sheet and the rubric is on its back, so each sheet has to go in face-up, on top of that student’s work.
Blank backs are kept and filed. A blank side is the proof that the scanner caught the page and that the student’s second side really was empty — so nothing is thrown away, and the count of pages filed always matches the count scanned.
